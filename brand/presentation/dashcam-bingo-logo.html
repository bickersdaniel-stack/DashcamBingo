What we heard

The brief

A bingo game a group of Aussie mates plays while one of them screen-shares dashcam videos on Discord. The logo has to work as a browser-tab icon, beside the name in the site's dark top bar, and as a Discord avatar, in one colour too, because every player picks their own square colour.

cheekysocialquickbold

Success: says dashcam and bingo without a lens, a ball or a red record dot; recognisable at 16 px; works on the site's charcoal and in one colour.

Final design

Viewfinder

The dashcam's frame locks onto your winning line.

  • Two frame corners say camera; a 3 × 3 card with a marked diagonal says bingo, with no lens, ball or record dot
  • Bingo Green is the site's own default square colour, so the logo and the game share one signal colour
  • A simplified cut (heavier corners, no dots) keeps it crisp from 32 px down to a 16 px browser tab
  • The name is set in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold, the site's own font, outlined so it looks the same everywhere
